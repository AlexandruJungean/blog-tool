## Key Takeaways Draft
> **Key Takeaways**
> - Private Czech lessons in Prague typically run 350 to 575 CZK an hour, roughly $15 to $25 (AmazingTalker, Language International, 2026).
> - Many expats start their search in Facebook groups or word of mouth, not because they lack Czech contacts, but because repeating the same ask every time they need something gets tiring.
> - A tutor operating regularly and charging for lessons should hold a trade license (živnostenský list); EU citizens can teach directly, non-EU residents generally need one first (expats.cz, TEFL Org, 2026).
> - Checking reviews matters: 93% of consumers read reviews before trusting a local provider (BrightLocal, 2025).

- Cover: why this matters for the person hiring, not just the tutor, it's a basic legitimacy signal
- **Image**: none, keep this section text-led
