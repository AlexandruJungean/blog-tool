## TL;DR Draft
> **TL;DR:** The Czech heating season runs September 1 to May 31; heat kicks in once the average daily temperature stays below 13°C for two consecutive days (Decree 194/2007 Sb.). Before then: get your boiler serviced (tenant's cost under the Civil Code, but the initial safety revision at move-in is the landlord's), check your flue was swept on schedule (once a year for gas appliances, up to three times a year for solid fuel, Decree 34/2016 Sb.), and know that radiators themselves and the shared heating pipes are the landlord's responsibility, not yours. Skipping the chimney/flue check isn't just a fire risk, insurers can cut or deny a claim without proof it was done.

### H2: When does heating season actually start? (200–300 words)
- **Answer-first**: The heating season runs September 1 to May 31 by law; heat is switched on once the average daily temperature drops below 13°C for two consecutive days (Decree 194/2007 Sb.).
- Cover: this is a rule, not a guess, buildings on district heating (roughly a third of Czech dwellings, more in Prague) follow it automatically; flats with their own boiler can start earlier if the tenant chooses.
- **Key stat**: 13°C threshold, two consecutive days, Decree 194/2007 Sb.

### H2: Boiler service: is it required, and who pays? (300–400 words)
- **Answer-first**: A routine annual service/inspection isn't a blanket legal requirement for households (it is for businesses, every 3 years under Nařízení vlády 191/2022 Sb.), but most manufacturers make it a warranty condition, and under the Civil Code it counts as tenant-paid "ordinary maintenance," separate from the landlord's one-time safety revision at move-in.
- Cover: the difference between "revize" (safety/gas-leak check, landlord's initial duty) and "servisní prohlídka" (annual servicing, tenant's ongoing cost); why skipping it risks the warranty and, per industry sources, can affect insurance payout after a fire or CO incident.
- **Image**: technician servicing a wall-mounted boiler.
- **Internal link** to /apartment-maintenance-landlord-vs-yours-2026 for the general repair-cost split.

### H2: Chimney and flue sweeps: the rule most renters miss (300–400 words)
- **Answer-first**: Under Decree 34/2016 Sb., gas-appliance flues need sweeping and inspection once a year (once every two years for condensing gas boilers); solid-fuel flues need sweeping up to three times a year if used continuously. Only a licensed kominík can certify it.
- Cover: why this matters even in a gas-heated flat, why it's usually bundled with the boiler service, why an insurer can reduce or refuse a payout without proof of a recent sweep.
- **Infographic**: simple frequency table by fuel type (gas vs. solid fuel), sourced to the decree.
- **Key stat**: gas 1x/year (or 1x/2yrs for condensing), solid fuel up to 3x/year.

### H2: Radiators and heating pipes: usually not your bill (250–350 words)
- **Answer-first**: The radiators themselves and the shared distribution pipes are the landlord's responsibility, not a tenant minor repair, under Nařízení vlády 308/2015 Sb.
- Cover: what counts as tenant-paid "small repairs" in this category (thermostatic valve replacement, minor cleaning) versus landlord-paid (a leaking radiator, a broken pipe); the current minor-repair cap (1,500 CZK per item, 150 CZK/m²/year total, as of Jan 2026).
- **[UNIQUE INSIGHT]** callout: "cold radiator" is not automatically your problem, check which part is actually broken before paying anyone.

### H2: How to bleed a radiator yourself (before calling anyone) (200–300 words)
- **Answer-first**: A radiator that's cold at the top but warm at the bottom almost always just needs bleeding, a five-minute fix, not a service call.
- Cover: simple steps (radiator key or flathead screwdriver, bleed valve, towel, listen for the hiss then a steady water stream), when this is enough versus when it signals a bigger problem (whole flat cold, boiler pressure dropping).
- **Image**: radiator thermostatic valve close-up.

- **Answer-first**: Demand for heating technicians spikes hard once temperatures drop, booking in August or early September gets you a slot; waiting until the first cold snap means a longer wait.

### FAQ Section (4–5)
1. When does the heating season start in the Czech Republic? — September 1 through May 31; heat switches on below 13°C average for two consecutive days (Decree 194/2007 Sb.).
2. Do I have to pay for my own boiler service? — Usually yes, it's tenant-paid ordinary maintenance; the landlord only covers the one-time safety revision at move-in.
3. Who pays if my radiator itself is broken? — The landlord; radiators and heating pipes aren't tenant minor repairs (Nařízení vlády 308/2015 Sb.).
4. How often does my chimney/flue need sweeping? — Once a year for gas appliances (every two years for condensing boilers), up to three times a year for solid fuel (Decree 34/2016 Sb.).
5. What if my radiator is cold at the top? — Bleed it first, it's usually trapped air, not a broken part.
