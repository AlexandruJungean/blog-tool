A quote in one line, "bathroom repair, 15,000 CZK," looks like the fast option. In reality, it's the most common reason a customer goes quiet, or picks a different tradesperson who wrote them more.

A more detailed quote doesn't have to mean more work. Just a different structure. And the difference in approval rates is significant.

> **Key Takeaways**
> - The more detailed your quote, the higher the chance a customer approves it on the first try, an itemized breakdown builds more trust than a single lump sum (vyfakturuj.cz, 2026)
> - A quote needs a clearly stated validity period, typically 7 to 30 days, otherwise it's effectively open-ended and reads as less trustworthy (vyfakturuj.cz, pajskr.cz, 2026)
> - Standard repairs and renovations to housing fall under the reduced 12% VAT rate, not the standard 21%, state the correct rate or note clearly that you're not VAT-registered (ainvoice.cz, taxorio.cz, 2026)
> - How fast you send a quote often matters more than the price itself, whoever reaches out first with a clear offer has a real advantage (LeadResponse, 2026)

## What must a price quote always include?

A price quote must include clear identification of both parties, your business ID and registered address, a precise and detailed scope of work or materials, a completion timeline, and terms of cooperation (vyfakturuj.cz, [fakturoid.cz](https://www.fakturoid.cz/almanach/zacatky-podnikani/cenova-nabidka), 2026). This isn't a formality, it's exactly what gives the customer confidence.

![Close-up of a document with a calculator on a desk](https://images.pexels.com/photos/7688524/pexels-photo-7688524.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&w=1200)

The more precisely you describe what the customer gets for their money, the fewer misunderstandings you'll deal with later. An itemized breakdown, material and labor listed separately with unit prices, reads far more trustworthy than a single number with no explanation.

---

## How long should a quote stay valid?

A price quote's typical validity is 7 to 30 days, without a stated date the quote is effectively open-ended, and that tends to bother customers more than they let on (vyfakturuj.cz, [pajskr.cz](https://pajskr.cz/jak-napsat-cenovou-nabidku/), 2026). Without a validity date, you also have no way to respond if material prices rise in the meantime.

For jobs dependent on material costs, a validity date matters even more, a two-month-old quote on materials with volatile pricing can easily put you at a loss. One sentence at the end of the quote is all it takes to cover yourself.

## What VAT rate should you state on the quote?

Standard repairs and renovations to housing fall under the reduced 12% VAT rate, not the standard 21% rate (ainvoice.cz, [taxorio.cz](https://www.taxorio.cz/blog/sazby-dph-2026/), 2026). If you're not VAT-registered, simply state that clearly on the quote and list prices without tax.

## Why does a more detailed quote beat the cheapest one?

The more precisely you describe what the customer gets for their money, the higher your chance they approve the quote on the first try, instead of asking more questions or comparing you against competitors (nejremeslnici.cz, 2026). Detail signals you actually thought through the job, not that you guessed a number off the top of your head.

The difference shows immediately side by side. "Bathroom repair, 15,000 CZK" tells the customer nothing about what's included. A broken-down version, with materials, labor, and timeline listed separately, gives them a clear picture and less reason to hesitate.

## FAQ

**What must a price quote always include?**

Business ID and contact details, a precise breakdown of work and materials, a completion timeline, and a validity period. A more detailed quote has a significantly higher chance of approval on the first try.

**How long should a quote stay valid?**

Typically 7 to 30 days, stated as a specific date. Without a date, the quote is effectively open-ended and reads as less professional.

**What VAT rate should I state on a home repair quote?**

In most cases 12%, the reduced rate for housing repairs and renovations, not the standard 21%.

**Do I need to state VAT if I'm not VAT-registered?**

No, simply state on the quote that you're not VAT-registered and list prices without tax.
